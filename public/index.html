How it works

📱

1 · Scan

Every table has its own QR code. Scanning opens that table's menu — no typing, no app install.

🛒

2 · Order

Browse by category, pick sizes and extras, add a note for the kitchen, then send.

👨‍🍳

3 · Kitchen

The ticket appears instantly on the kitchen display, colour-coded by how long it has been waiting.

🍲

4 · Served

Guests watch the status live, call a waiter, or ask for the bill with one tap.
